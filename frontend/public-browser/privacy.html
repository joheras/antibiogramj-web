Privacy policy

AntibiogramJ web version (https://joheras.github.io/antibiogramj-web/) · Last updated 17 November 2026

AntibiogramJ is a free, open-source tool to measure and interpret disk-diffusion antibiograms. The web version runs entirely in your browser. This page explains what happens to your data. The source code is public at github.com/joheras/antibiogramj-web.

Data processed in your browser

  • Photos of plates are analysed on your own device. They are not sent to any AntibiogramJ server: there is none. The developers do not receive your images, results or any other data.
  • Experiments, images, results and the disk codes the app learns are stored in your browser's storage (IndexedDB) on your device. You can delete them at any time from the app or by clearing the site data.
  • The app does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Google Drive (optional)

If you click Connect Google Drive, the app loads Google's sign-in library from accounts.google.com and asks Google for access with the permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file only. With it, AntibiogramJ can see, create and change only the files that it has created itself in your Drive; it cannot read your other files.

  • What is stored: the backups you save (experiments, plate images and results) and the photos you send from your phone in Capture mode with the organism, strain, sample and notes you type. Everything is stored in the folder AntibiogramJ of your own Google Drive, and nowhere else.
  • How it is used: only to provide the features you use: saving and loading backups, and moving photos from your phone to your computer. Your browser talks to Google directly; no one else receives the data.
  • Account data: the app reads the e-mail address of the Google account only to show which account is connected. It is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Tokens: the access token given by Google is kept in memory only while the page is open and expires after about one hour. No passwords or refresh tokens are stored.
  • Sharing: the data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or to train models. AntibiogramJ's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Removing access: click Disconnect in the app, or remove AntibiogramJ at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The files in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

Patient data

Do not enter names or other data that identify patients. Use the sample codes of your laboratory. You are responsible for following the data-protection rules of your institution when you store images or results in your browser or in your Google Drive.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. For questions, open an issue at github.com/joheras/antibiogramj-web/issues.
